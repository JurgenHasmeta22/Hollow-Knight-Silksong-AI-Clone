# THE RESONANCE VEIL
**Genre:** 2D Dark Wuxia Action-RPG / Revenge Thriller  
**Logline:** In an eyeless world where every living soul wears a psychic mask to perceive reality through telepathic sight, a betrayed swordsman modifies his own mask into an unreadable blank void—hunting down the elite Order that slaughtered his clan.

---

## World Lore: The Masks of Mind-Sight
Humanity evolved past physical eyes ages ago, retaining only speech, hearing, and scent—alongside a latent telepathic sense. However, raw telepathic perception is chaotic and unfocused on its own. To navigate the world, **everyone wears a mask**.

* **Masks as Psychic Lenses:** Forged from resonant minerals, bone, or lacquered wood, masks act as neural amplifiers and filters. Putting on a mask snaps the mind into **Mind-Sight**—allowing people to perceive spatial geometry, read incoming movements, and sense the emotional aura of others while speaking and smelling normally.
* **Mask Hierarchy:** A person’s mask defines their place in society. Commoners wear rough, porous clay masks with limited perceptual range, while artisans, trackers, and warriors commission specialized masks tuned to sharpen specific senses—like tracking blood-scent across miles or reading micro-vibrations in the air.
* **The Unmasked Curse:** Stripping someone of their mask doesn't hide them—it plunges them into agonizing sensory blindness and psychic vertigo. In this world, taking a warrior's mask is a fate worse than death.

---

## The Antagonists: The Sovereign Concord
At the apex of society rules **The Sovereign Concord**, an authoritarian order of elite sword-masters who monopolize the rarest psychic ores to forge **Grand Resonator Masks**.

* **Overwhelming Telepathic Sight:** Through their elite masks, the Concord’s masters perceive a battlefield in 360-degree slow motion. They read an opponent's muscle tension, heartbeat, and attack intent before the blade even leaves its scabbard.
* **Psychic Suppression:** The elites weaponize their masks to broadcast crushing waves of telepathic static—shattering the Mind-Sight of lesser rebels and forcing them to fight blind while the Concord cuts them down effortlessly.

---

## The Vengeance Story: Vael & The Blank Mask
**Vael** was once a sworn blade-brother of a loyalist clan that guarded the Concord’s deepest ore mines. When his clan discovered the Order was harvesting human minds to power their Grand Resonators, they rebelled—and were annihilated in a single night by the Concord’s high commanders.

Left for dead with his original clan mask shattered, Vael crept into the forbidden depths of the mines and forged a new faceplate from **Null-Obsidian**—creating a completely smooth, featureless white mask with zero resonance markings.

* **Turning Their Sight Against Them:** While every other mask in the world acts like a beacon that sends and receives telepathic signals, Vael’s **Blank Mask** acts like a psychic black hole. It gives him razor-sharp Mind-Sight while refusing to bounce any signal back. To the Concord’s elite swordsmen, Vael appears as a terrifying, unreadable smear in their telepathic vision.
* **Weaponizing the Void:** By feeding stolen telepathic energy into his blank mask, Vael can fracture his silhouette into **Phantom Clones** that fool enemy masks, trigger **Aura Flares** that momentarily blind an elite's Mind-Sight, and unleash **Dissonant Transformations**—overloading his own senses to move faster than a master’s mind can process.

---

## Core Gameplay & Combat Hooks
* **Mask-Break Executions:** Instead of standard stagger meters, breaking an enemy swordsman's posture cracks their mask—temporarily blinding their telepathic sight and opening them up to lethal dual-blade finishers.
* **Stealing Mask Arts:** Defeating each elite commander allows Vael to absorb fragments of their shattered Grand Resonator into his blank mask, unlocking new special attacks (like whirlwind dashes, energy barrages, and spectral summons).
* **Sensory Disruption:** Powerful bosses can emit psychic interference that distorts the player's own screen—forcing you to rely on directional sound cues, spoken enemy taunts, and scent-trails until your mask recalibrates.
